Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Overview

All In Wake ("the App") is an iOS wake-up challenge app. At the scheduled time, a local notification invites you to open the App and play a roulette game to stop the alarm sound. Your privacy is important to us.

Data We Access

Notifications: The App requests permission to send local notifications so it can alert you at alarm times. Notification content stays on your device and is scheduled by iOS.

Data We Do NOT Collect

The App does not:

  • Collect, store, or transmit personal data to our servers
  • Require an account or registration
  • Use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools
  • Access your contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone
  • Involve real-money gambling

Local Storage

Alarm settings (time, label, weekdays, sound preference, enabled state) are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage (UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party analytics SDKs or advertising networks in this version.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
